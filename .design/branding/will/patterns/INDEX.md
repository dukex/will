## Quick reference

**Brand:** Sage (70%) + Creator (30%) — opinionated tool, zero decoration

**Colors:** `#000000` background · `#0D0D0D` card · `#4F6EF7` accent (interactive only) · `#E05252` destructive · `#4CAF74` success

**Type:** Geist Sans (content) + Geist Mono (data labels) · max weight 500 · never 700

**Structure:** 1dp `#2A2A2A` borders · 0dp radius on task cards · no shadows · no gradients

**Voice:** Precise, dry, uncompromising, peer-to-peer — no exclamation marks, no emoji, sentence case

**Key constraints:** no engagement mechanics ever · no wellness language · edge-to-edge, no app chrome · dark-only for MVP
